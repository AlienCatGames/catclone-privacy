Catclone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Catclone has no ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't track you for advertising or analytics.

What is stored online

  • Your account: your username and a player ID. Your password is handled by Unity's sign-in service; we never see it. Without an account you play as a guest with just a player ID.
  • Progress and settings saved to the cloud (coins, items, stats, looks), so they follow you to other devices.
  • Your public profile: name, level, title, outfit and game stats, which other players can see.
  • Leaderboard scores and replays (ghosts) of your best runs.
  • Friends: your friends list and friend requests, and whether you're online and in which lobby, so friends can join you.
  • Things you share: maps, Community Shop items (with images you add), clips and notes on bars.
  • Moderation: reports you send about other players, and when you were last online.
  • Chat messages go to the players in your lobby. They're not saved on our servers.

Who handles it

Everything is stored by Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies), which runs Catclone's accounts, saves, lobbies and leaderboards. See Unity's privacy policy.

We only use your data to run the game and to keep it safe, for example acting on reports.

Safety

Chat is filtered. You can mute or block players and report players, maps and Community Shop items. Admins review reports and can remove content or ban players.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings > PRIVACY & SAFETY > DELETE MY ACCOUNT. This removes your account, cloud save, profile, leaderboard scores, friends, shared maps and Community Shop items.
  • Progress kept on your own device stays there until you uninstall the game.
  • By email: you can also ask us to delete your data at aliencatgamessupport@gmail.com.

Children

If you're a parent and want your child's data removed, email us and we'll delete it.
